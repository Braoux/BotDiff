# About BotDiff — shareable product brief

[← BotDiff](../README.md)

## One-line pitch

**EN:** BotDiff helps League of Legends players prepare a botlane 2v2 with a practical matchup-specific game plan.

**FR :** BotDiff t'aide à préparer ton 2v2 botlane sur League of Legends avec un plan de jeu concret adapté aux quatre champions.

## Short introduction

**EN:** Pick your ADC, your support and the opposing duo. BotDiff gives you a structured view of the lane: early levels, trade windows, wave management, threats and target priority. The product is currently in alpha.

**FR :** Sélectionne ton ADC, ton support et le duo adverse. BotDiff te propose une lecture structurée de ta lane : premiers niveaux, trades, wave, menaces et cibles prioritaires. Le produit est actuellement en alpha.

## Positioning

- **For:** players who want to understand and prepare a botlane matchup.
- **Core promise:** a more actionable way to think about the entire 2v2.
- **Not:** a guarantee of victory, an official Riot product or a source of infallible strategic advice.
- **Current stage:** alpha.

## Communication guidelines

Please distinguish gameplay suggestions from established game facts. Don't claim every matchup is supported, every recommendation is correct, the product is generally available, or that BotDiff is endorsed by Riot Games.

Use official Riot / League of Legends materials only in accordance with the applicable Riot policies and rights. No third-party artwork, footage or user testimonials are included in this repository.

**Note:** Implementation details, prompts, datasets, system architecture, keys, metrics and internal roadmap are not part of the public communications kit.

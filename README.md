## The matchup is more than two champions

You know your champion. Maybe you know the enemy ADC. But what happens when **all four kits** meet in the same lane?

BotDiff helps you prepare for that specific 2v2. Choose your ADC and support, then the opposing duo, and explore a practical lane plan instead of trying to piece it together during loading screen.

## What you get

| 🎯 | Your plan | What it helps you decide |
| :---: | --- | --- |
| ⚡ | **Early levels** | What to look for at levels 1, 2 and 3 |
| 🤝 | **Trade windows** | When to contest a fight — and when to respect the enemy |
| 🌊 | **Wave management** | How the minion wave affects your options |
| 🏹 | **Target priority** | Who to focus when a fight breaks out |
| 🛡️ | **Threats and mistakes** | Which enemy tools and risky plays to watch for |
| 🔄 | **Post-level-6 adjustments** | How the lane may change once ultimates are available |

Insights are matchup-specific **guidance, not guaranteed outcomes**. League of Legends changes regularly, and gameplay recommendations should be evaluated critically.

## How it works

1. **Select four champions** — your ADC, your support, their ADC and their support.
2. **Request your analysis** — BotDiff prepares an overview of the 2v2.
3. **Read the lane plan** — start with the tactical summary, then go deeper into your options.
4. **Take it into game** — use the advice as a starting point and adapt to what actually happens.
